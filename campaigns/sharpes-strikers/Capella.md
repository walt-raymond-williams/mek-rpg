## Overview

Capella is the seventh planet of a binary-star system in the Capellan Confederation. It is one of the foundational worlds of Capellan history: settled in 2193 by Jurdan Aris and his followers, it became the seat of the Capellan Holdfast, the Capellan Republic, the Capellan Co-Prosperity Sphere, the Capellan Hegemony, and then the Capellan Commonality before the Confederation was founded in 2367. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing Handbook: House Liao, pp. 12-13, 17; House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), pp. 12-13, 28.

Politically, Capella remains important because it is the administrative capital of the modern Capella Commonality and a historical center of Capellan legitimacy even though the national capital moved to Sian after Franco Liao's destruction of the Davion expedition on Capella Prime. Do not confuse the modern Capella Commonality administrative region with the pre-Confederation Capellan Commonality state founded in 2310 and dissolved in July 2367. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing Objectives: Capellan Confederation, p. 8, and House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), p. 28; Sarna: Capellan Commonality, citing Handbook: House Liao, p. 13.

Historically, Capella is the old heart of Capellan state formation. Its early rulers made knowledge, learning, and the arts strategic assets, and the world became known for scholarship, information exchange, and commercial influence before the Confederation existed. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing Handbook: House Liao, p. 13, and House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), p. 13.

Economically, Capella combines financial, industrial, and shipping importance. The Capellan Commonality Bank is headquartered in Herald and functions as the Confederation's state-owned central bank, currency authority, financial planner, and major source of loans and credit. Ceres Metals Industries is headquartered in Ceres City, and Rashpur-Owens maintains major aerospace and shipbuilding facilities tied to Capella's orbital infrastructure. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Capellan Commonality Bank, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), pp. 118-119, and Handbook: House Liao, pp. 129-131; Sarna: Ceres Metals Industries; Sarna: Rashpur-Owens Incorporated.

Militarily, Capella is a protected core world with strategic industry, training facilities, an A-class HPG, zenith and nadir recharge stations, and a long garrison history. Blandford's Grenadiers were stationed on Capella in 3025, 3028, 3040, and 3050; the Capella page also lists them on Capella in 3038. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), p. 105; Historical: War of 3039, p. 140; 20 Year Update, p. 62; Sarna: Blandford's Grenadiers.

Industrially, Capella matters because Ceres Metals, Rashpur-Owens, Mujika Aerospace, Capella University Press, and past or destroyed shipbuilding concerns are tied to the world or system. Ceres and Rashpur-Owens facilities at Capella's L1 point are specifically identified as major aerospace manufacturing infrastructure. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing Objectives: Capellan Confederation, p. 8; Sarna: Ceres Metals Industries; Sarna: Rashpur-Owens Incorporated; Sarna: Mujika Aerospace Technologies.

Capella's relationship to House Liao is institutional and symbolic. House Liao rules the Confederation through the Chancellorship, and Capella's early House Aris history predates the Liao Confederation, but after 2367 Capella is part of the Liao state. The Capellan Commonality Bank has traditionally had a House Liao member as director, tying Capella's financial apparatus directly to the ruling house. Source: Sarna: Capellan Confederation; Sarna: Capellan Commonality Bank; Sarna: Capella.

Capella remains important despite Sian becoming the national capital because Sian is the seat of the Chancellor and central government, while Capella remains an ancestral state-forming world, Commonality administrative capital, financial headquarters, military training site, industrial node, and heavily trafficked jump-point system. Canon supports this division of function; this file treats Capella as a historical and institutional capital, not the Confederation's national capital after 2367. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Capellan Confederation.

### Jump Point Operations

JumpShips normally operate at standard zenith and nadir points above and below the system ecliptic because those points minimize gravity complications and simplify safe navigation. A JumpShip carries DropShips on docking hardpoints, remains at or near the jump point, recharges its K-F drive after arrival, and depends on DropShips for in-system passenger and cargo movement. Source: Sarna: JumpShip, citing Strategic Operations, pp. 88-90 and 134-135, and other sources listed there.

The 6.19-day travel time means Capella's system traffic has a real operational lag. A DropShip leaving Capella VII cannot reach a JumpShip at the standard point instantly; customs decisions, military clearances, passenger transfers, inspection disputes, and urgent dispatches all unfold across days unless a narrative uses nonstandard points or special circumstances. Source: Sarna: Capella.

## Recharge Stations

### Canon Summary

Capella has both zenith and nadir recharge stations in the thirty-first century. Canon gives their existence, position type, and system recharge time, but the Capella article does not name station commanders, harbor masters, engineering chiefs, customs officers, CCAF liaisons, commercial tenants, or named residents. Source: Sarna: Capella.

Recharge stations exist to speed or regularize JumpShip K-F drive charging at jump points. JumpShips normally recharge with their jump sails over roughly six to nine days depending on stellar class; recharge stations collect power with their own solar sails and store it in batteries, then transfer power to docked or connected JumpShips or beam it to a deployed jump sail. Source: Sarna: JumpShip; Sarna: Recharge Station / Space Station, citing BattleSpace, p. 21, Strategic Operations, p. 126, and DropShips and JumpShips - Operations Manual, p. 34.

Recharge operations must protect the K-F drive. Microwave transfer still requires a long charging period to avoid K-F damage; a physical docking or cable connection can shorten charging compared to remote beamed transfer. Hot-loading through a vessel power plant can reduce recharge time but risks K-F damage and misjump consequences, so a reputable Capellan station should treat it as an emergency or military-authorized procedure rather than routine commerce. Source: Sarna: JumpShip.

Docking operations involve JumpShips, DropShips, small craft, station-keeping requirements, jump sails, and large exclusion distances around active K-F operations. JumpShips must have DropShips secured on K-F booms before jump, and vessels should keep other craft clear before jump execution. Source: Sarna: JumpShip.

Civilian traffic would logically include merchant JumpShips, passenger DropShips, industrial cargo, Ceres Metals shipments, Rashpur-Owens components, banking couriers, civilian spacers, and station services. This is an inference from Capella's canon economy and JumpShip operations, not a canon traffic manifest. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Ceres Metals Industries; Sarna: Rashpur-Owens Incorporated; Sarna: JumpShip.

Military traffic would logically include CCAF movements, Blandford's Grenadiers logistics during the 3025-3050 period, aerospace patrols, military couriers, and industrial-security movements to and from L1 facilities. This is an inference from Capella's canon military and industry, not a canon order of battle for the stations. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Blandford's Grenadiers.

Maintenance capabilities are not specified for Capella's recharge stations. Canon generally supports power transfer, docking/cable operations, and station logistics for recharge stations, but does not state that Capella's stations can perform major JumpShip or K-F-drive overhauls. For major repairs, Capella has nearby strategic shipbuilding and aerospace industry, and Rashpur-Owens manufactures JumpShips and K-F drives on Capella, but station-level repair limits are not specified. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: JumpShip; Sarna: Rashpur-Owens Incorporated.

No canon staff are named for either Capella recharge station.

Daily life aboard the station is disciplined but lived-in. The station wakes by watch rotation rather than by day/night cycle. Public corridors smell of sealant, hot oil, noodles, starch coffee, and ozone from power-transfer hardware. The most important public displays show collar assignments, recharge estimates, customs holds, CCAF priority notices, shuttle departures, and a quiet red-bordered safety board listing K-F exclusion warnings. Merchants open around incoming emergence signatures, not local time.

The station has three social layers. The command and engineering layer is formal, Capellan, and safety-focused. The commercial layer is transactional and multilingual, with station merchants watching for ships that need food, parts, crew rotation, legal help, or debt restructuring. The transient-spacer layer is intimate and rumor-rich: JumpShip crews, DropShip crews, mercenaries, couriers, industrial techs, and passengers spend enough time trapped together that grudges and friendships can last for years.

The station is a plausible long-term home port because JumpShips repeatedly return to known recharge stations, station personnel remember reliable crews, and Capellan authorities value mercenary commands that respect port law. Sharpe's Strikers can build trust here by keeping manifests clean, treating station workers well, responding to emergencies, and not forcing Capellan officers to choose between law and politics.

If a K-F drive repair exceeds station capability, the campaign default is to refer the vessel to the Aris Memorial Yards in the Necromo system when era-appropriate and available. For the 3035-3036 campaign period, this is a campaign simplification and should be treated as restricted CCAF referral rather than routine commercial routing: canon identifies Necromo as a secret DropShip repair facility and JumpShip shipyard important to the Confederation, gives Necromo no recharge stations in 3025, and places its zenith/nadir recharge stations from the late 3030s to 3071. Later canon describes major expansion and public unveiling in the 3050s. Source boundary: Sarna: Necromo; Sarna: Aris Memorial Yards.

## Planet

Capella VII, commonly called Capella, is the seventh planet in the system and has one moon, Nikos. It has 1.02 surface gravity, standard breathable atmosphere, 60 percent surface water, and a very hot equatorial temperature of 38 C. Its highest native life is plant life. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), p. 143; Handbook: House Liao, p. 71; Objectives: Capellan Confederation, p. 8.

The planet has two named continents: Llagarres and Pociello. Canon describes the world as hot by Terran standards but does not provide a detailed climate-zone map, ocean names, mountain ranges, rivers, biomes, or agricultural regions. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing Objectives: Capellan Confederation, p. 8.

From orbit, canon supports the following visual anchors: a very hot habitable world, 60 percent water coverage, two major continents, one moon, and significant orbital/L1 aerospace infrastructure. Any specific ocean color, cloud pattern, city-light distribution, agricultural belt, or visible landmark beyond these facts is campaign interpretation unless verified later. Source: Sarna: Capella.

Transportation canon is sparse. The system's standard jump-point travel time is 6.19 days from Capella VII, which implies routine DropShip transfer between the planet and jump points. The L1 aerospace facilities imply regular orbital-to-planet industrial movement. Ground transportation details are not specified. Source: Sarna: Capella.

## Major Cities

### Memorial City

Memorial City is the planetary capital on Llagarres. Canon names it as the capital but does not provide a detailed district map, government-building list, architecture guide, mayor, police organization, or population. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing Objectives: Capellan Confederation, p. 8.

Government: Memorial City should be treated as the seat of Capella's planetary administration and the Diem of Capella unless later canon specifies a different administrative location. The Capella page names the political leader title as Diem of Capella and the capital as Memorial City. The standalone Diem page identifies Anurios Liao as Diem in 3025; the reviewed canon does not confirm the 3035-3036 office holder. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Diem of Capella, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), p. 143.

Architecture: Canon does not describe Memorial City's architecture. Campaign-safe depiction should emphasize official memorialization, Capellan state symbolism, heat-conscious public spaces, old academic/civic institutions, and security-conscious government architecture without claiming specific canon structures.

Districts: Canon does not name Memorial City districts. Use original district names only under Campaign Interpretation or scene notes.

Industry: Canon does not identify Memorial City as the industrial center; Ceres City and L1 facilities are the stronger industrial anchors. Memorial City can host government procurement, legal offices, archives, and service industries as inference, not canon.

Military presence: Capella's garrison history and capital status imply security, but canon does not name Memorial City garrison bases. Use Blandford's Grenadiers, planetary militia, CCAF transit forces, and capital security as system-level facts, not city-map facts. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Blandford's Grenadiers.

Culture and landmarks: The name Memorial City invites ceremonial and commemorative use, but the reviewed canon does not specify landmarks. The destruction of Capella Prime and the black border added to the Confederation insignia are system-defining memorial facts and can shape Memorial City's public culture if clearly marked as inference. Source: Sarna: Capella, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), p. 28.

### Herald

Herald is a city on Pociello. It contains the Capellan Commonality Bank and the Capella War College. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Capellan Commonality Bank; Sarna: Capella War College.

The Capellan Commonality Bank was founded on 5 January 2450 and is headquartered in Herald. It is the state-owned central bank and financial planner of the Confederation, handles financial interactions inside the Confederation and with other Successor States, holds state securities, helps determine the yuan's value, provides loans and credit, and works with the Ministry of Trade and Exchange on currency exchange and trade negotiations. Source: Sarna: Capellan Commonality Bank, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), pp. 118-119, and Handbook: House Liao, pp. 129-131.

The Capella War College was founded in 2270 and is the oldest military academy in the Capellan Confederation. It trains MechWarriors, aerospace fighter pilots, DropShip and JumpShip crews, vehicle crews, armored infantry, and conventional infantry. Students frequently cross-train with the Duchy Regional Training Center to support CCAF combined-arms doctrine. Graduation Days occur every six months and are local holidays with parades, martial demonstrations, historical reenactments, and air shows. War College alumni may wear a jian pin, and the Holdfast Guard receives first pick of graduates. Canon does not name the 3035-3036 commandant or provide a campus map in the reviewed sources. Source: Sarna: Capella War College, citing Field Manual: Capellan Confederation, p. 46; Handbook: House Liao, p. 105; Objectives: Capellan Confederation, p. 8.

Government significance: Herald's banking headquarters gives it national financial importance; this is stronger than ordinary municipal status. Any claim that Herald is the seat of a particular ministry, court, or Commonality office beyond the bank is not established in the reviewed sources.

Military significance: Herald's War College makes it a training and prestige center. Use it for scenes involving cadets, instructors, doctrinal debates, military examinations, staff officers, and CCAF professional networks, but do not invent named faculty as canon.

### Ceres City

Ceres City is a notable city on Llagarres and the site of the planet-based Ceres Metals Industries plant. Source: Sarna: Capella.

Ceres Metals Industries has its headquarters in Ceres City on Capella VII. It was founded in 2322, became one of the Inner Sphere's most successful conglomerates, and is best known as a military armaments manufacturer with broad holdings, including civilian ground vehicles through Ceres Motors. Source: Sarna: Ceres Metals Industries, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), pp. 119-120, and Handbook: House Liao, pp. 132-133.

Manufacturing: Ceres Metals is associated with aerospace fighters, battle armor, BattleMechs, conventional fighters, OmniMechs, vehicles, and many components. The Capella page specifically links Ceres City to the planet-based Ceres Metals plant, while the Ceres Metals page identifies corporate headquarters there. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Ceres Metals Industries.

Workers: Canon does not give Ceres City's labor population, union structure, housing, or worker culture. Scenes may infer a dense skilled-industrial workforce, corporate security, CCAF procurement oversight, and subcontractor neighborhoods, but those are campaign interpretation.

Orbital connections: Capella's L1 aerospace facilities include Ceres Metals operations. Ceres City should have strong orbital logistics connections by inference, but canon does not name a specific space elevator, spaceport, shuttle line, or orbital cargo authority. Source: Sarna: Capella.

### Rashpur-Owens Incorporated

Rashpur-Owens was founded on 2 January 2698, is affiliated with the Capellan Confederation, is headquartered in Herald on Capella VII, and produces DropShips and JumpShips. After early Succession War devastation, it was the Confederation's only functional orbital construction yard capable of building JumpShips, based out of former Delhi WarShips facilities on Capella. In the 3035-3036 campaign era, do not portray the yard as modern or healthy: Sarna describes it before Riley Doh's 3051 modernization as costly, decrepit, and disintegrating, even though it remained a symbol of Capellan pride. Source: Sarna: Rashpur-Owens Incorporated, citing Masters and Minions: The StarCorps Dossiers, p. 58; Handbook: House Liao, p. 135; BattleSpace, p. 28; Objectives: Capellan Confederation, p. 8.

By 3079, the Capella facility had suffered no Jihad losses of capital or personnel and operated at 80 percent production volume. It was also in a crash construction program to build repair docks for remaining Capellan WarShips. These are later-era facts, useful for long-term continuity but not directly true of the 3035-3036 campaign date. Source: Sarna: Rashpur-Owens Incorporated, citing Objectives: Capellan Confederation, p. 3, and Field Report: CCAF, p. 6.

For 3030s play, use Rashpur-Owens as a major strategic shipbuilding and aerospace-industrial presence, but do not import 3079 repair-dock conditions into the 3035-3036 era unless the campaign deliberately foreshadows future capacity.

### Ceres Metals Industries

Ceres Metals has corporate headquarters in Ceres City, Capella VII. It is a broad military-industrial conglomerate, one of the first developers of BattleMech technology, and a cornerstone of Capellan military-hardware production. Ceres operates across many worlds; do not assume every Ceres product or component listed on Sarna is built on Capella. Source: Sarna: Ceres Metals Industries.

Ceres products and components include BattleMechs, OmniMechs, aerospace fighters, conventional fighters, vehicles, armor systems, engines, communications systems, and other military hardware. The Vindicator is especially associated with the CCAF. Source: Sarna: Ceres Metals Industries.

For scene generation, Ceres City and the Ceres plant should feel like a major defense-industrial environment: skilled workers, corporate engineers, military procurement officers, industrial security, CCAF inspectors, cargo yards, restricted production floors, component subcontractors, and labor pressure from state planning. Worker life specifics remain campaign interpretation unless later sourced.

### Mujika Aerospace Technologies

Mujika Aerospace Technologies is a Capellan and later Lyran-linked aerospace company headquartered on St. Ives III, with a manufacturing center on Capella. In the late thirty-first century it was one of the Capellan Confederation's leading defense contractors specializing in conventional and aerospace fighters. Source: Sarna: Mujika Aerospace Technologies.

### Blandford's Grenadiers

Blandford's Grenadiers later became known as the Holdfast Guard. The unit originated from Tikonov Union Guards formations and was reorganized in 2368 for the newly formed CCAF, where it was used against private noble armies resisting Franco Liao. Source: Sarna: Blandford's Grenadiers, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), p. 78, and Field Report 2765: CCAF, p. 7.

During the Star League era, Blandford's Grenadiers were one of the experienced Capellan Hussars regiments. In 3025 the unit was a BattleMech regiment rated Elite/Fanatical, commanded by Colonel Richard De Salvo, with principal BattleMechs including Catapults and Thunderbolts, and was stationed on Capella. Source: Sarna: Blandford's Grenadiers, citing House Liao (The Capellan Confederation), pp. 78 and 105.

The unit remained on Capella during the Fourth Succession War according to the Sarna unit page, though that specific statement is marked citation-needed there. The Capella page independently lists Blandford's Grenadiers at Capella for 3025, 3038, 3040, and 3050. Source: Sarna: Blandford's Grenadiers; Sarna: Capella.

During the Andurien/Canopus conflict, Blandford's Grenadiers retook Sigma Mare in March 3032, took Palladaine in November 3034 without firing a shot, and returned to their Capella home garrison by 3035. In July 3039, the Ninth Marik Militia raided Capella, surprised the Grenadiers, inflicted heavy casualties, and forced them to abandon the planet after two days of limited resistance. Source: Sarna: Blandford's Grenadiers, citing Historical: Brush Wars, pp. 63, 66, 79, 87, and Historical: War of 3039, pp. 63 and 140.

## Government

Capella's canon government fields include a Noble Ruler entry for the Duke of Capella and House Aris from 2193 to 2270, a Political Leader title of Diem of Capella, and a capital at Memorial City. Anurios Liao is the only named Diem found in reviewed Sarna pages, listed for 3025. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Diem of Capella.

The Capellan Commonality was founded in 2310, dissolved in July 2367, had Capella as capital world, and had a Prime Minister as head of state. It was the successor to the Capellan Hegemony. Source: Sarna: Capellan Commonality, citing Handbook: House Liao, p. 13.

The Capellan Confederation was founded in July 2367, has Sian as capital, uses the yuan, is ruled by the Chancellor, and has the CCAF and Capellan Confederation Navy as military arms. Source: Sarna: Capellan Confederation.

Relationship to Sian: Sian is the national capital after Franco Liao moved the capital there. Capella remains a Commonality capital and historical/industrial/financial center. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Capellan Confederation.

Relationship to the Chancellor: Capella is subject to the Chancellor's state authority, centralized planning, CCAF strategic control, and House Liao political order. It is not the Chancellor's seat after the capital moved to Sian. Source: Sarna: Capellan Confederation; Sarna: Capella.

Civilian administration: Canon names the Diem of Capella as political leader and Anurios Liao as Diem in 3025, but does not name the office holder for 3035-3036 in the reviewed sources. Treat local ministries, port officials, customs offices, and municipal officers as campaign interpretation unless later sourced.

## Economy

Capella's economy combines:

- Financial authority through the Capellan Commonality Bank in Herald. Source: Sarna: Capellan Commonality Bank.
- Defense industry through Ceres Metals, Rashpur-Owens, Mujika Aerospace, L1 facilities, and related manufacturers. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Ceres Metals Industries; Sarna: Rashpur-Owens Incorporated; Sarna: Mujika Aerospace Technologies.
- Interstellar shipping through zenith/nadir recharge stations and heavy JumpShip/DropShip traffic implied by core-world industry. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: JumpShip.
- Academic and publishing activity through Capella's old knowledge-center identity and Capella University Press, a Capella-based academic publisher active as of 2759. Source: Sarna: Capella; Sarna: Capella University Press.

Exports likely include military hardware, aerospace components, financial services, technical expertise, educational/publishing products, and industrial goods. This is an inference from named canon industries, not a canon export ledger.

Imports likely include raw materials, specialty components, food or climate-sensitive agricultural inputs, luxury goods, replacement parts, and off-world labor or experts. This is an inference; canon does not provide an import table for Capella.

The Confederation economy is centrally planned, with economic goals set by the Chancellor and Ministry Planning Committee every four years. During the 3030s, state pressure on production and transport is especially severe; Romano Liao made wartime economic restriction more formal after 3036, and in 3040 issued sweeping property-control decrees. Source: Sarna: Capellan Confederation, citing Handbook: House Liao, pp. 106-108, and Masters and Minions: The StarCorps Dossiers, p. 59.

For 3035-3036 campaign play, Capella should feel like a major industrial and financial core world under wartime scarcity pressure: efficient, bureaucratic, proud, suspicious of legal irregularities, and capable of redirecting resources quickly when the state decides a command or ship matters.

## Campaign Interpretation

This section contains original setting material intended to make Capella usable for scenes while staying consistent with canon.

### System Feel

Capella should feel like an old, proud, hot, crowded, disciplined core system where history is never background. Arrivals at the zenith point see orderly Capellan port control, CCAF traffic priority, commercial impatience, lawyers and customs officers moving quickly, and enough industry on the manifests to remind every spacer that Capella builds things the Confederation needs.

The system's emotional tone in 3035-3036 is post-disaster survival and hardening. The Fourth Succession War hurt the Confederation badly; Capella is not ruined, but it is under pressure. Officials are alert to fraud, foreign manipulation, smuggling, industrial espionage, and noble power plays. Competent outsiders can earn respect, but nobody gets casual trust at a core Capellan strategic node.

### Memorial City Scene Defaults

Use Memorial City for official hearings, Diem-level paperwork, government patronage, public ceremonies, high-security archives, House Liao symbolic politics, and formal meetings where everyone understands that Capella remembers its founding sacrifices.

### Capella Zenith Recharge Station As Home Port

Use the station as a recurring ensemble setting. It should have enough continuity that a character can know which stall sells decent noodles, which customs officer is fair but relentless, which engineer can be trusted with a drive diagnostic, and which bar will be full of Rashpur-Owens techs after second watch.

Persistent story hooks:

- A customs lien or seizure order may be technically valid but politically manipulated.
- A CCAF liaison may protect the Strikers because they are useful, while still recording every irregularity.
- A Ceres or Rashpur-Owens engineer may need discreet mercenary help with industrial espionage or stolen components.
- A ComStar clerk may know which message was delayed, misrouted, or paid for twice.
- A JumpShip captain may beg for priority charge after a misjump scare or passenger emergency.
- A station merchant may hear rumors before planetary intelligence does.
- A young War College graduate may arrive at the station convinced doctrine has all the answers.
- A future 3039 security failure can be foreshadowed by harmless-looking Marik traffic and complacent confidence in Capella's core-world safety.

## Campaign Canon

This section contains campaign-specific lore and should be treated as true only for this campaign.

Beginning around 3035-3036, Sharpe's Strikers establish the Capella Zenith Recharge Station as their primary home port.

The command maintains good relations with station personnel and the CCAF.

The recharge station becomes the unit's recurring logistical hub.

Crews develop long-term professional and personal relationships with station personnel.

The station serves as the primary location for JumpShip inspections, K-F drive diagnostics, maintenance planning, crew rotation, leave, and logistics.

If K-F drive repairs exceed station capabilities, vessels are referred through restricted CCAF channels to the Aris Memorial Yards on Necromo when the facility is available and when secrecy or CCAF clearance permits referral. This is campaign canon for Sharpe's Strikers; the exact 3035-3036 referral process is not established by reviewed canon.

For campaign tone, the Capella Zenith Recharge Station should become a recurring setting comparable in narrative function to Deep Space Nine or Babylon 5: a familiar location with recurring NPCs, evolving relationships, commerce, politics, and story hooks that persist across decades of campaign time.

The station is not neutral ground in the legal sense. It is a Capellan installation in a Capellan core system, but it is commercially practical, used to outsiders, and accustomed to the uneasy peace required by interstellar trade.

Sharpe's Strikers' good standing does not make them immune to inspection. It means station personnel are more likely to explain the problem, give the command a chance to fix paperwork, or quietly signal which office must be satisfied before a crisis becomes public.
