## Active Factions

### Long Transit Association

- Table role: Civilian transport cooperative seeking permanent charter protection with Sharpe's Strikers.
- Current attitude toward PCs: Cautious hope. They chose the Strikers because the unit is independent, proven, and not large enough to erase the cooperative's identity.
- Agenda: Preserve the `Jade Passage`, `Celestial Garden`, crews, family ownership shares, passenger business, and legal standing.
- Known assets: Merchant-class JumpShip `Jade Passage`; Monarch-class DropShip `Celestial Garden`; experienced civilian crews; incomplete evidence of debt manipulation.
- Important NPCs: Lian Zhou, Captain Mei Ren, Captain Tomas Vale, Chief Engineer Aron Vesk.
- Obligations, debts, or leverage: Offers docking collars, transport, passenger/headquarters capacity, and civilian revenue share. Needs military escort, operating support, legal defense, and Capellan-recognized protection.
- Open canon questions: Exact cooperative charter paperwork, debt amounts, port jurisdictions, and noble identity remain to be established in play.

### Capellan Regional Noble Behind The Seizure

- Table role: Political/legal antagonist for `The Jade Passage`.
- Current attitude toward PCs: Indirectly hostile if the Strikers interfere with the impoundment or debt seizure.
- Agenda: Force Long Transit Association to sell its last vessels below value or absorb them into a private fleet.
- Known assets: Influence over local licenses, ports, creditors, and customs paperwork.
- Important NPCs: Name and title TBD.
- Obligations, debts, or leverage: Can create legal trouble without openly attacking a Capellan-recognized mercenary command.
- Open canon questions: Identity, evidence trail, jurisdiction, and whether Maskirovka attention is personal, political, or incidental.

### Capellan Confederation

- Table role: Primary long-running employer and political patron/risk for Sharpe's Strikers.
- Current attitude toward PCs: Inferred strongly favorable but transactional. MekHQ exposes 8 completed Capellan contracts, all Success, plus the active `3031 - CC - Adler Planetary Assault` contract. No source-confirmed numeric faction standing or reputation score is exposed by the current live API.
- Contract evidence: 9 of 10 total MekHQ contracts are with the Capellan Confederation when including the active Adler contract; 8 of 9 completed contracts are Capellan successes. The only non-Capellan contract is `3027 - Magistracy of Canopus - Butzfleth Pirate Hunting`, also Success.
- Agenda: Use the Strikers as a proven mercenary instrument for raids, garrison work, relief duty, and planetary assault operations while keeping contract control through Liaison or House command terms.
- Known assets: Repeated transport compensation and battlefield support; current Adler contract includes 100% transport compensation, 70% salvage, and 60% battle loss compensation.
- Important NPCs: Sang-wei Qiao Ren remains the named Capellan liaison from the earlier Mule/Carried Interest thread unless superseded.
- Obligations, debts, or leverage: The Strikers have a long success ledger with the Confederation, giving Sharpe real bargaining leverage for DropShip support, title cleanup, transport-credit offsets, or conditional use rights. The Capellans still have command-right leverage and can attach strings to any settlement.
- Open canon questions: Exact Capellan faction standing, reputation score, employer satisfaction, and MRBC-style rating are not exposed by the current live API and should not be invented.
