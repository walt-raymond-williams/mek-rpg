## Active Factions

### Long Transit Association

- Table role: Civilian transport cooperative seeking permanent charter protection with Sharpe's Strikers.
- Current attitude toward PCs: Cautious hope. They chose the Strikers because the unit is independent, proven, and not large enough to erase the cooperative's identity.
- Agenda: Preserve the `Jade Passage`, `Celestial Garden`, crews, family ownership shares, passenger business, and legal standing; eventually recover `Vermilion Gate` if the disputed lien can be suspended or defeated.
- Known assets: Merchant-class JumpShip `Jade Passage`; Monarch-class DropShip `Celestial Garden`; experienced civilian crews; incomplete evidence of debt manipulation. GM/planning knowledge: proposed second Merchant-class JumpShip `Vermilion Gate` under lien, not yet revealed to Sharpe unless established in play.
- Important NPCs: Tora Wichers, Captain Damiane Meyer, Captain Nadežda Dunajski, Chief Engineer Damon Dimas.
- Obligations, debts, or leverage: Offers docking collars, transport, passenger/headquarters capacity, and civilian revenue share. Needs military escort, operating support, legal defense, Capellan-recognized protection, and help turning the `Vermilion Gate` lien into a payable retainer instead of a seizure.
- Open canon questions: Exact cooperative charter paperwork, port jurisdictions, noble identity, true lawful debt, fraudulent debt portion, and `Vermilion Gate` condition remain to be established in play.

### Capellan Regional Noble Behind The Seizure

- Table role: Political/legal antagonist for `The Jade Passage`.
- Current attitude toward PCs: Indirectly hostile if the Strikers interfere with the impoundment or debt seizure.
- Agenda: Force Long Transit Association to sell its vessels below value or absorb them into a private fleet; preserve the `Vermilion Gate` lien as leverage even if `Jade Passage` survives the first challenge.
- Known assets: Influence over local licenses, ports, creditors, lien paperwork, and customs orders.
- Important NPCs: Name and title TBD.
- Obligations, debts, or leverage: Can create legal trouble without openly attacking a Capellan-recognized mercenary command. The claimed `900,000,000 C-Bill` lien on `Vermilion Gate` is the preferred long-term pressure tool.
- Open canon questions: Identity, evidence trail, jurisdiction, fraudulent debt portion, and whether Maskirovka attention is personal, political, or incidental.

### Capellan Confederation

- Table role: Primary long-running employer and political patron/risk for Sharpe's Strikers.
- Current attitude toward PCs: Inferred strongly favorable but transactional. MekHQ exposes a long run of successful Capellan work plus the active `3034 - CC - Lesalles Recon Raid` contract. No source-confirmed numeric faction standing or reputation score is exposed by the current live API.
- Contract evidence: Current live API shows the active Capellan contract as `3034 - CC - Lesalles Recon Raid`; historical Capellan contracts remain a major part of the Strikers' success ledger. The non-Capellan `3027 - Magistracy of Canopus - Butzfleth Pirate Hunting` contract was also a success.
- Agenda: Use the Strikers as a proven mercenary instrument for raids, garrison work, relief duty, and planetary assault operations while keeping contract control through Liaison or House command terms.
- Known assets: Repeated transport compensation and battlefield support; current Lesalles contract includes 100% transport compensation, 40% salvage, 100% straight support, and a MekHQ transport amount of 0 C-Bill because the Strikers are carrying themselves.
- Important NPCs: Sang-wei Qiao Ren remains the named Capellan liaison from the earlier Mule/Carried Interest thread unless superseded.
- Obligations, debts, or leverage: The Strikers have a long success ledger with the Confederation, giving Sharpe real bargaining leverage for DropShip support, title cleanup, transport-credit offsets, conditional use rights, or `Vermilion Gate` lien suspension. The Capellans still have command-right leverage and can attach strings to any settlement, including first-refusal contract pressure and a narrative `10,000,000 C-Bill` lien credit per qualifying Capellan contract completed after conditional release.
- Open canon questions: Exact Capellan faction standing, reputation score, employer satisfaction, and MRBC-style rating are not exposed by the current live API and should not be invented.
